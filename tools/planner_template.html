Import picks up your party and every PC box. Pick File A, B or C.rxdata from %APPDATA%\infinitefusion (Hoenn: %APPDATA%\infinitefusion-hoenn); paste that path into the file window's address bar. The file is read in your browser and never uploaded.

Already-fused Pokémon are split into their two parts. Tick or untick a box to include it, and click a Pokémon's name to change its level, IVs, nature or evolution.

    Team style

    Each fusion gets three scores from 0 to 100: Stats (attacking stat, bulk, Speed), Typing (weaknesses, resistances, immunities) and Moves (how hard its best four attacks hit across all types). The style decides how much each counts.

    Best overall counts all three equally. Hard hitters leans on stats and moves. Bulky & safe leans on typing, including resistances. Fewest weaknesses only cares about avoiding weaknesses (a 4× weakness counts double) and steers the whole team away from weak spots. Custom lets you set the balance; only the ratio matters, so 10/10/10 is the same as 1/1/1.

    Many fusions score within a few points of each other, so switching styles can swap several picks.

    Level and training

    Each Pokémon uses its own level and IVs. A fusion's level is (2 × higher + lower) ÷ 3, as in the game. That level decides its stats, its level-up moves and whether it has evolved. Its IVs are the average of both parents'.

    Same level for everyone ignores individual levels so you can compare fusions fairly. EV-trained adds 252 EVs to its two most useful stats.

    Options

    Evolved forms: fusions evolve when either part does. The planner tries every stage the fusion's level allows, including middle ones (Elekid, Electabuzz and Electivire), and uses whichever fuses best. Level evolutions wait for the level; stone and trade evolutions count right away. Every stage shows up under "Every pairing".

    Hidden abilities: when off, only the two abilities a fusion normally gets are counted.

    Egg moves: egg moves that also have a TM always count; this adds the rest.

    No repeats: avoids the same species twice in a team, even if you own two. A Magikarp counts as the Gyarados it will become.

    Custom sprites: gives a small boost to fusions that have a hand-drawn sprite in the game.

      How it works

      Every number comes from the game's own data files (version __PIF_VERSION__): base stats, types, abilities, level-up moves, TM and tutor compatibility, and evolutions. Fusions are calculated with the same formulas the game uses. A fusion takes two thirds of the head's HP, Sp. Atk and Sp. Def and two thirds of the body's Attack, Defense and Speed, plus a third of the other parent's. Its IVs are the average of both parents' IVs, and its nature is one of the two parents' natures.

      Each fusion gets three scores. Stats rewards a high attacking stat, bulk and Speed. Typing counts weaknesses, resistances and immunities, including abilities like Levitate. Moves looks at the four attacks that hit the most types hardest. The recommended team is the combination with the best total that also avoids sharing the same weaknesses.

      Imported saves are read entirely in your browser. Scores are a planning guide. They don't account for held items, status moves or AI behaviour.
